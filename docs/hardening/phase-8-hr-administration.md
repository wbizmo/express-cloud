# Phase 8 — HR, administration and configurability

- Departments, job roles, employee profiles, assignments, attendance, holidays and performance reviews.
- Payroll tables and service are present but disabled by default until statutory configuration is deliberately enabled.
- Maker-checker administration covers branches, warehouses, customers, payment methods, departments and employees.
- Makers cannot approve their own changes; every decision requires a note.
- Core records support active/inactive lifecycle transitions rather than destructive deletion.
